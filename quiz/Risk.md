### Q3. `rsk-002` — 單選題

**題目 ID**: `rsk-002`

難度: `medium` ｜ 建議作答時間分類: `calculation` ｜ 版本標籤: `pmbok7` ｜ 原始 performanceDomain: `Risk` ｜ ECO domain: `Process`

**題幹**

- EN: A risk has a 25% probability of occurring. If it occurs, it will cause a $80,000 cost overrun. What is the Expected Monetary Value (EMV) of this risk?
- 中: 某風險發生機率為 25%,一旦發生將造成 8 萬美金的成本超支。此風險的期望貨幣價值(EMV)為多少?

**選項**

- (a) -$5,000 / -$5,000
- (b) -$20,000 / -$20,000 ✅ **正解**
- (c) -$80,000 / -$80,000
- (d) $20,000 / $20,000

**詳解**

EMV = 機率 × 衝擊金額 = 25% × (-$80,000) = -$20,000。因為是威脅(負面風險),EMV 為負值,代表預期損失金額。

### Q4. `rsk-003` — 單選題

**題目 ID**: `rsk-003`

難度: `hard` ｜ 建議作答時間分類: `calculation` ｜ 版本標籤: `pmbok8` ｜ 原始 performanceDomain: `Risk` ｜ ECO domain: `Process`

**題幹**

- EN: A project faces a risk with a 40% probability of causing a $100,000 loss. The PM is considering a mitigation action costing $15,000 that would reduce the probability to 10% (the impact stays at $100,000 if it still occurs). Compare the net expected value of "do nothing" versus "mitigate," and determine which option the PM should choose.
- 中: 專案面臨一項風險,有 40% 機率造成 10 萬美金損失。PM 正考慮花費 1.5 萬美金採取減緩措施,可將發生機率降至 10%(若仍發生,衝擊金額不變)。請比較「不採取行動」與「採取減緩措施」的淨期望值,並判斷 PM 應選擇哪個方案。

**選項**

- (a) Do nothing: net EV = -$40,000; choose to do nothing / 不採取行動:淨期望值 -$40,000,應選擇不採取行動
- (b) Mitigate: net EV = -$25,000; choose to mitigate / 採取減緩措施:淨期望值 -$25,000,應選擇減緩 ✅ **正解**
- (c) Mitigate: net EV = -$15,000; choose to mitigate / 採取減緩措施:淨期望值 -$15,000,應選擇減緩
- (d) Both options have equal expected value / 兩者期望值相同,選哪個都可以

**詳解**

不採取行動 EMV = 40% × (-$100,000) = -$40,000。採取減緩措施淨期望值 = 減緩成本 $15,000 +剩餘風險期望值(10% × $100,000 = $10,000)= -$25,000。因為 -$25,000 優於(損失小於)-$40,000,故應選擇採取減緩措施。

### Q6. `rsk-005` — 單選題

**題目 ID**: `rsk-005`

難度: `easy` ｜ 建議作答時間分類: `definition` ｜ 版本標籤: `pmbok8` ｜ 原始 performanceDomain: `Risk` ｜ ECO domain: `BusinessEnvironment`

**題幹**

- EN: An organization states: "We are generally willing to accept moderate risk in pursuit of higher returns, but any single risk causing more than $500,000 in loss must trigger escalation." Which two risk-related terms does this statement illustrate, respectively?
- 中: 某組織聲明:「我們整體上願意為追求更高報酬而承擔中等程度的風險,但任何單一風險若造成超過 50 萬美金損失,必須觸發升級機制。」此聲明分別說明了哪兩個風險相關名詞?

**選項**

- (a) Risk Appetite, then Risk Threshold / 風險胃納(Risk Appetite),接著是風險門檻(Risk Threshold) ✅ **正解**
- (b) Risk Tolerance, then Risk Exposure / 風險容忍度(Risk Tolerance),接著是風險曝險(Risk Exposure)
- (c) Risk Threshold, then Risk Appetite / 風險門檻(Risk Threshold),接著是風險胃納(Risk Appetite)
- (d) Risk Exposure, then Risk Tolerance / 風險曝險(Risk Exposure),接著是風險容忍度(Risk Tolerance)

**詳解**

「整體上願意承擔中等風險」描述的是組織對風險的整體態度傾向,即風險胃納(Risk Appetite);「超過 50 萬美金必須觸發升級」則是可量化的具體界線,即風險門檻(Risk Threshold)。

### Q8. `rsk-007` — 單選題

**題目 ID**: `rsk-007`

難度: `hard` ｜ 建議作答時間分類: `calculation` ｜ 版本標籤: `pmbok8` ｜ 原始 performanceDomain: `Risk` ｜ ECO domain: `Process`

**題幹**

- EN: The PM is comparing two contracting options for a subsystem. Option A (fixed-price in-house build) costs $200,000 with certainty. Option B (outsourcing) has a 50% chance of costing $150,000 and a 50% chance of costing $280,000. Which option has the lower expected cost, and by how much?
- 中: PM 正在比較兩個子系統的採購方案。方案 A(內部固定價格建置)確定成本為 20 萬美金。方案 B(外包)有 50% 機率成本為 15 萬美金,50% 機率成本為 28 萬美金。哪個方案的期望成本較低?低多少?

**選項**

- (a) Option A is lower by $15,000 / 方案 A 較低,低 1.5 萬美金 ✅ **正解**
- (b) Option B is lower by $15,000 / 方案 B 較低,低 1.5 萬美金
- (c) Option A is lower by $30,000 / 方案 A 較低,低 3 萬美金
- (d) Both options have equal expected cost / 兩方案期望成本相同

**詳解**

方案 B 期望成本 = 50% × $150,000 + 50% × $280,000 = $75,000 + $140,000 = $215,000。方案 A 為確定成本 $200,000。$215,000 − $200,000 = $15,000,故方案 A 期望成本較低 1.5 萬美金。

### Q14. `rsk-013` — 拖拉配對題

**題目 ID**: `rsk-013`

難度: `medium` ｜ 建議作答時間分類: `definition` ｜ 版本標籤: `pmbok7` ｜ 原始 performanceDomain: `Risk` ｜ ECO domain: `Process`

**題幹**

- EN: Drag and match each threat (negative risk) response strategy to its corresponding opportunity (positive risk) counterpart that shares the same underlying logic.
- 中: 請將下列每一種「威脅(負面風險)」回應策略,拖曳配對到採用相同邏輯的對應「機會(正面風險)」策略。

**配對項目(拖曳來源)**

- p1: Avoid — eliminate the risk cause entirely / 避免(Avoid)——徹底消除風險成因
- p2: Transfer — shift ownership of the risk to a third party / 轉移(Transfer)——將風險責任轉移給第三方
- p3: Mitigate — reduce the probability or impact / 減輕(Mitigate)——降低發生機率或衝擊
- p4: Accept — take no proactive action, or reserve contingency / 接受(Accept)——不主動處理或保留應變準備金
- p5: Escalate — report beyond the PM's authority / 上呈(Escalate)——上報超出 PM 職權範圍的風險

**選項池(拖曳目標)**

- c1: Exploit — ensure the opportunity definitely happens / 利用(Exploit)——確保機會一定發生
- c2: Share — partner with a third party to jointly capture the benefit / 分享(Share)——與第三方合作共享收益
- c3: Enhance — increase the probability or impact / 增強(Enhance)——提高發生機率或衝擊
- c4: Accept — take no proactive action, or capture the benefit if it occurs / 接受(Accept)——不主動處理,機會發生時再受益
- c5: Escalate — report beyond the PM's authority / 上呈(Escalate)——上報超出 PM 職權範圍的機會

**✅ 正確配對**

- p1 → c1: Avoid — eliminate the risk cause entirely → Exploit — ensure the opportunity definitely happens（避免(Avoid)——徹底消除風險成因 → 利用(Exploit)——確保機會一定發生）
- p2 → c2: Transfer — shift ownership of the risk to a third party → Share — partner with a third party to jointly capture the benefit（轉移(Transfer)——將風險責任轉移給第三方 → 分享(Share)——與第三方合作共享收益）
- p3 → c3: Mitigate — reduce the probability or impact → Enhance — increase the probability or impact（減輕(Mitigate)——降低發生機率或衝擊 → 增強(Enhance)——提高發生機率或衝擊）
- p4 → c4: Accept — take no proactive action, or reserve contingency → Accept — take no proactive action, or capture the benefit if it occurs（接受(Accept)——不主動處理或保留應變準備金 → 接受(Accept)——不主動處理,機會發生時再受益）
- p5 → c5: Escalate — report beyond the PM's authority → Escalate — report beyond the PM's authority（上呈(Escalate)——上報超出 PM 職權範圍的風險 → 上呈(Escalate)——上報超出 PM 職權範圍的機會）

**詳解**

威脅與機會的五種回應策略彼此一一對應,採用相同邏輯但方向相反:Avoid↔Exploit(徹底消除↔確保發生)、Transfer↔Share(轉移責任↔共享收益)、Mitigate↔Enhance(降低↔提高機率衝擊);Accept↔Accept 與 Escalate↔Escalate 則是威脅與機會共用、名稱相同的策略。
