### Q71. `stk-069` — 點擊熱區題

**題目 ID**: `stk-069`

難度: `easy` ｜ 建議作答時間分類: `definition` ｜ 版本標籤: `pmbok8` ｜ 原始 performanceDomain: `Stakeholders` ｜ ECO domain: `Process`

**題幹**

- EN: Click the card that best describes the primary purpose of a project Kickoff Meeting.
- 中: 請點選最能描述專案「啟動會議(Kickoff Meeting)」主要目的的卡片。

**熱區選項**(方括號是畫面上的座標百分比,新增題目不填也沒關係,匯入時會自動排版)

- (n1) Formally launch the project, align stakeholders on objectives, and build initial team commitment / 正式啟動專案,使利害關係人對目標達成共識,並建立團隊初步的承諾感 ✅ **正解** [x=5,y=5,w=40,h=35]
- (n2) Review detailed technical designs line-by-line for approval / 逐行審查詳細技術設計以取得核准 [x=52.5,y=5,w=40,h=35]
- (n3) Negotiate the final contract price with a vendor / 與供應商協商最終合約價格 [x=5,y=47.5,w=40,h=35]
