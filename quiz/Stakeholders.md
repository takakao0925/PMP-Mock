### Q1. `mr-001` — 多選題

難度: `medium` ｜ 建議作答時間分類: `agile_scenario` ｜ 版本標籤: `pmbok8` ｜ 原始 performanceDomain: `Stakeholders` ｜ ECO domain: `People`

**題幹**

- EN: Which two of the following are key practices for effective stakeholder engagement? (Select two.)
- 中: 下列哪兩項是有效利害關係人參與(Stakeholder Engagement)的關鍵作法?(請選擇兩項)

**選項**

- (a) Communicate with stakeholders only at project start and closeout / 僅在專案啟動與結束時與利害關係人溝通
- (b) Continuously assess changes in stakeholder engagement levels / 持續評估利害關係人的參與程度變化 ✅ **正解**
- (c) Tailor communication strategy based on stakeholder power and interest / 依利害關係人的影響力與利益調整溝通策略 ✅ **正解**
- (d) Treat all stakeholders with the same communication approach / 將所有利害關係人一律用同一種溝通方式對待

**詳解**

有效的利害關係人參與需要持續監控其參與程度,並依據影響力/利益調整客製化的溝通與互動策略。

### Q2. `match-002` — 拖拉配對題

難度: `easy` ｜ 建議作答時間分類: `definition` ｜ 版本標籤: `pmbok7` ｜ 原始 performanceDomain: `Stakeholders` ｜ ECO domain: `People`

**題幹**

- EN: Drag and match each stakeholder engagement strategy to the scenario it best fits.
- 中: 請將下列利害關係人參與策略拖曳配對到對應的情境描述。

**配對項目(拖曳來源)**

- p1: Monitor / 監控(Monitor)
- p2: Keep Informed / 隨時告知(Keep Informed)
- p3: Keep Satisfied / 保持滿意(Keep Satisfied)
- p4: Manage Closely / 密切管理(Manage Closely)

**選項池(拖曳目標)**

- c1: Low power, low interest — monitor with minimal effort / 低影響力、低利益,僅需最小程度關注
- c2: Low power, high interest — keep informed regularly / 低影響力、高利益,需定期提供資訊
- c3: High power, low interest — keep satisfied to avoid unwanted interference / 高影響力、低利益,需維持其滿意度以免介入干預
- c4: High power, high interest — manage closely with frequent collaboration / 高影響力、高利益,需密切合作與頻繁溝通

**✅ 正確配對**

- p1 → c1: Monitor → Low power, low interest — monitor with minimal effort（監控(Monitor) → 低影響力、低利益,僅需最小程度關注）
- p2 → c2: Keep Informed → Low power, high interest — keep informed regularly（隨時告知(Keep Informed) → 低影響力、高利益,需定期提供資訊）
- p3 → c3: Keep Satisfied → High power, low interest — keep satisfied to avoid unwanted interference（保持滿意(Keep Satisfied) → 高影響力、低利益,需維持其滿意度以免介入干預）
- p4 → c4: Manage Closely → High power, high interest — manage closely with frequent collaboration（密切管理(Manage Closely) → 高影響力、高利益,需密切合作與頻繁溝通）

**詳解**

此為利害關係人權力/利益方格(Power/Interest Grid)四個象限對應的典型參與策略。
